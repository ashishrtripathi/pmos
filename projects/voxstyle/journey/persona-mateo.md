| **5. Audio Design** | Mix SFX & voiceover | Generate ultra-clean VO, add cinematic sound effects, adjust EQ | Robotic sounding voices | Audio Mixer |
| **6. Multi-format Export** | Render for all channels | Batch export 9:16, 16:9, and 1:1 with lossless quality | Slow renders and compression artifacts | Export Hub |
