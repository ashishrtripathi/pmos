### Journey Steps (Left → Right)

| Step | Activity | Tasks | Pain Points | Screen |
|------|----------|-------|-------------|--------|
| **1. Campaign Brief** | Set up target audience & hook | Input campaign goals, target demographics, and key selling propositions | Writing high-converting hooks takes too much trial and error | Campaign Studio |
| **2. AI Ad Variation** | Generate 5 visual hooks | Auto-generate multiple hook variations and CTA end-cards for A/B testing | Single ad fatigue burns marketing budget | Hook Generator |
| **3. Brand Compliance** | Enforce brand guidelines | Apply company colors, typography, logos, and disclaimer copy automatically | Agency ads frequently miss legal copy requirements | Brand Guard |
| **4. Fast Rendering** | Render 4K ad variations | Cloud render all aspect ratios (9:16 vertical, 16:9 widescreen, 1:1 square) | Slow local rendering bottlenecks launch deadlines | Ad Renderer |
| **5. Direct Publishing** | Connect ad platforms | Push directly to TikTok Ads Manager and Meta Business Suite with tracking UTMs | Manual uploading and metadata entry wastes hours | Ad Publisher |
| **6. Performance Analytics** | Monitor CTR & ROAS | Track retention curves and identify best-performing video scenes | Hard to pinpoint exact drop-off seconds in video ads | Performance Dashboard |
